## Brand & Style

This design system embodies modern romantic intimacy. Built specifically for couples cherishing shared memories, milestones, and daily notes, the aesthetic fuses contemporary minimalism with delicate, warm romance. It sidesteps kitsch and excessive sentimentality in favor of an airy, serene, and deeply emotional editorial atmosphere.

### Personality & Tone
- **Intimate & Loving:** Soft, tender, and vulnerable without feeling juvenile.
- **Airy & Editorial:** Generous breathing room, deliberate negative space, and curated compositions that feel like a high-end personal keepsake journal.
- **Pure & Warm:** Rooted in warm alabaster, gentle rose gold, and deep romantic crimson touches that ground the softer pastel tones.

### Visual Style
A harmonious fusion of **Soft Warm Minimalism** and **Frosted Romantic Glassmorphism**. Layouts favor squircle containers, translucent milky surfaces over warm ambient gradients, feather-soft tinted shadows, and feather-light borders that mimic delicate parchment edges.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around genuine warmth, intimacy, and legibility. It relies on a gentle foundation of cream-infused whites, soft blush tints, and rich velvet rose accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#BE3A5A` - Velvet Crimson):** A passionate, grounded rose-red. Used for critical interactive focal points, selected states, heart reactions, and key calls-to-action.
- **Secondary (`#C98C70` - Rose Gold / Terracotta Warmth):** A muted, luminous rose gold tone. Applied to badges, secondary interactions, memory milestone markers, and subtle decorative outlines.
- **Tertiary (`#F4D8CD` - Delicate Blush):** Soft powdered blush used for interactive hover fills, chip backgrounds, memory tags, and gentle tonal containers.
- **Neutral Base (`#FBF7F5` - Warm Cream Alabaster):** Replaces harsh stark white with an organic, linen-like comfort that softens contrast and makes photographic memories radiant.
- **Surface & Glass Tint (`rgba(255, 250, 248, 0.72)`):** Frosted glass panels layered with a subtle rosy cast and backdrop blur.
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - *Text Primary (`#2E1E24`):* Deep charcoal burgundy for warm, readable, high-contrast text.
  - *Text Muted (`#7B686E`):* Soft dusty mauve for metadata, timestamps, and secondary captions.
  - *Border Subtle (`rgba(190, 58, 90, 0.08)`):* Delicate crimson-tinted hairline borders.

## Typography

The type scale orchestrates a graceful balance between editorial romanticism and functional clarity.

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Evokes the timeless intimacy of love letters, bespoke wedding vows, and cherished hardbound photo books. It is used exclusively for memory titles, anniversary countdowns, quotes, and primary section headers.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Its rounded, humanist geometric shapes deliver effortless legibility, soft curves, and a clean modern counterweight to the serif headings.
- **Rhythm & Treatments:**
  - Headings should never feel condensed; ensure generous line heights to preserve the airy cadence.
  - Italics in `headline-md` and `headline-sm` may be selectively employed for quotes or deeply personal micro-notes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts prioritize comfortable intimacy over data density. Visual content—like shared photos, voice notes, and milestone cards—is treated as individual keepsakes.

### Layout Philosophy & Grid
- **Fluid Modular Grid:** 4 columns on mobile, 8 columns on tablet, and 12 columns on desktop. Max container width is pegged to `1120px` to maintain focused, editorial intimacy.
- **Asymmetric Keepsakes:** Masonry or alternating offset card arrangements mimic photo albums and scrapbook collages while maintaining strict baseline alignment.
- **Generous Bleed & Margins:** Edge spacing is kept generous (`margin-lg: 3.5rem` on desktop) to enclose memories gracefully. On mobile, `margin: 1rem` preserves usable surface area while maintaining softness.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is ethereal and tactile, avoiding flat starkness or heavy synthetic shadows.

### Atmospheric Layering
1. **L0 - Base Canvas:** Organic cream alabaster (`#FBF7F5`) with occasional faint radial gradient blooms of soft blush (`#F4D8CD` at 25% opacity) positioned behind key hero photos or milestones.
2. **L1 - Frosted Keepsake Surface:** Semi-opaque warm white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) with a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an ultra-subtle border (`1px solid rgba(190, 58, 90, 0.08)`).
3. **L2 - Elevated Interactive Card:**
   - Soft ambient rose shadow: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(190, 58, 90, 0.08), 0 2px 8px -1px rgba(46, 30, 36, 0.04)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` top-edge specular highlight.
4. **L3 - Modals & Floating Love Notes:**
   - Ambient drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(190, 58, 90, 0.12), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(46, 30, 36, 0.06)`
   - Full glass sheen effect with backdrop blur of `24px`.

## Shapes

The design system uses soft, organic squircle geometries (`roundedness: 2`). Sharp corners are eliminated entirely to invoke warmth, safety, and comfort.

- **Cards & Keepsake Frames:** 1rem to 1.5rem (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`) squircle radius, soft to the touch and framing images like bespoke Polaroid borders.
- **Pills for Intimate Badges:** Full circular/pill radius used strictly for counters (e.g., "Day 1,248 together"), status indicators, and heart action buttons.
- **Photo Cutouts:** Light organic arch shapes or softened squircles may be selectively used for couple avatars or featured anniversary headers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Love CTA):** Filled with primary Velvet Crimson (`#BE3A5A`), crisp white label (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, semi-bold), subtle inward glow, and gentle rose shadow. On tap/hover, subtle scale expansion (`scale(1.02)`) with a brief bloom shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Rose Gold Outline):** Bordered with a 1.5px soft rose gold tint (`#C98C70`), frosted warm background, and crimson-tinted text.
- **Icon / Reaction Button:** Circular or squircle pill button featuring heart, sparkle, or letter icons. Incorporates a spring-based scale animation and floating particle micro-interaction when toggled.

### Cards & Keepsake Tiles
- Built with rounded squircle containers (`rounded-xl`), framed with `1px` delicate crimson-hued borders.
- **Memory Card:** Displays a date header in `label-sm`, full-bleed image with softened inner corners, brief editorial caption in `Playfair Display`, and an optional audio/milestone tag.
- Cards maintain ambient pink-toned elevation on hover, lifting smoothly with a 200ms ease curve.

### Chips & Milestone Tags
- Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`) containers utilizing soft tertiary blush (`#F4D8CD` at 40% opacity) with a muted crimson text label.
- Used for memory categories (e.g., "First Trip", "Anniversary", "Late Night Thoughts", "Letterbox").

### Input Fields & Love Letters
- Rounded `0.75rem` text inputs and rich note fields.
- Cream surface fill (`#FFFFFF` on `#FBF7F5` canvas) with a faint warm border (`rgba(190, 58, 90, 0.15)`).
- When focused, border transitions to Velvet Crimson with a delicate `0 0 0 3px rgba(190, 58, 90, 0.1)` ambient glow.
- Placeholder text in soft dusty mauve (`#7B686E`).

### Checkboxes & Milestones (Bucket List Items)
- Checkboxes are softened rounded squares (6px radius) transitioning from a pale blush border to a filled Velvet Crimson background with a crisp white heart or checkmark glyph upon completion.
- Strikethrough text transitions smoothly with a faded mauve color shift.

### Dedicated Keepsake Components
- **Counter Capsule:** A pill-shaped floating widget showing total days together or countdowns, styled in frosted rose glass with bold serif numerals.
- **Sealed Note Accordion:** A clean envelope-inspired container that gently expands with an airy slide-down animation to reveal private letters and audio snippets.
